Am I Living Under a Rock?

Am I living under the rock
that Moses struck?

Were we playing a "Who’s the most important?" game?
Or was it a "Who’s the most remembered?" game?

Either way,
I definitely didn’t win those games.

Five years and two terms,
and yet—

here I am,
wondering how easily
a person can disappear
from the memories
of people they once knew.

Was I properly captured in the picture,
or was I standing at the side
and simply got cropped out?

Maybe I was never
as memorable
as I thought I was.

Maybe I was only
a small part
of a much bigger story.

But I thought
I would at least
be remembered.

I don’t know what hurts more:

being overlooked,

or realizing
how much it hurts
to be overlooked.

So much for being
so alive.

If I had known
this would happen,
maybe I would have cared
a little less
while I was there.

Hi, cameraman.

Please, take a new picture.

Or maybe,
for you reading this,
you don't need to take a new picture.

Just check the old one again.

You were there.

Somewhere in someone’s story,
you will be remembered.

Don’t worry.
